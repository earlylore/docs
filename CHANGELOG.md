## 2026-11-08

Public changelog label: v1.9, "8-Seat Orthogonal Argon Roundtable, 4-Lane Evaluation Pipeline & Brand Studio".

- The public changelog describes idea evaluation as a 4-lane pipeline: Deep uses an 8-seat orthogonal roundtable (4 domain experts and 4 buyers), and Standard uses a 4-seat screening with an in-place upgrade to Deep.
- `/design` adds the App Logo & Icon Studio and the AI naming studio. The changelog describes catalog collision checks and live multi-TLD DNS checks for names.
- Pro and Max quotas were calibrated. Current limits are on [/pricing](https://earlylore.com/pricing).

## 2026-11-07

Public changelog label: v1.8, "Dynamic Share Posters, Full-Spectrum Revenue Evidence & Research Guides".

- The public changelog says revenue evidence covers 10,200+ products in three tiers: verified MRR, verified 30-day/ARR payment feeds, and dated founder disclosures with the original quote. Other pages use other revenue populations; quote the page you mean.
- `/trending?tab=revenue` adds a revenue-disclosure feed.
- Public pages gain a share flow with 1200×630 Open Graph cards and 1080×1350 QR posters.
- Research guides are published at `/blog`, with `/changelog`, `/llms.txt`, and a bilingual `/sitemap.xml`.

## 2026-11-06

Public changelog label: v1.7, "6-Lane Argon Idea Evaluation Engine, MCP Server & Claude Code Skill".

- The public changelog describes the evaluation pipeline on this date as 6-lane, with 7 domain experts and 7 buyers. The 2026-11-08 entry describes a 4-lane pipeline. Both statements stay dated.
- The hosted HTTP MCP server is published at `/api/mcp`, with the guide at `/mcp` and the skill spec at `/skill.md`. The changelog names 11 tools, including `earlylore_search_products`, `earlylore_inspect_product`, `earlylore_get_evaluation`, `earlylore_get_design_dna`, and `earlylore_get_niche_benchmark`.
- Pay-as-you-go evaluation packs are listed from $1.9, alongside Pro and Max. The changelog describes a 7-day annual upgrade credit for pack purchases.
- Founder domain claim and Stealth Shield: the changelog says an owner can verify a domain with a DNS TXT record or a meta tag and hide traffic and revenue metrics from public pages.

## 2026-11-05

Public changelog label: v1.6, "9,700+ First-Fold Design DNA Profiles, 12 Style Playbooks & Trending Feed".

- `/design` publishes computed first-screen styles in 12 styles. The figure printed on this changelog entry is 9,700+. `/mcp` also prints 40,900+; this entry does not replace that other figure.
- Style pages include craft notes and a copyable design brief. Product pages include a Design DNA section.
- `/trending` tracks breakout posts and growth moves.
- The changelog describes desktop (1440×900) and mobile (390×844) first-screen snapshots, with color shares, font pairings, radius, and hero layout.

## 2026-11-04

Public changelog label: v1.5, "Research Notes Drawer, 1-on-1 AI Product Chats & Monthly Market Reports".

- The signed-in workspace is organized around chats, following, and `/notes`.
- Research notes can be saved from product pages, evaluation reports, and assistant answers, with a link back to the conversation.
- Monthly reports are published at `/reports`.
