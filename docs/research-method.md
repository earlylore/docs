# Research method

Source: <https://earlylore.com/#method> · 中文: <https://earlylore.com/zh/>

A useful product research page answers three questions: **what was observed, when it was checked, and what the evidence can support.**

## What a research record contains

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Observed object | The product and its official site |
| Signal | Revenue evidence, or "unpublished" when the product has not disclosed it |
| Change to follow | Pricing, positioning and features |
| Evidence | Source, date and the change itself |

Records are re-checked when the source moves. An observation is kept separate from any conclusion drawn from it.

## What EarlyLore does and does not claim

- It helps you test an idea against real products, categories, pricing, creators and source evidence.
- It does **not** promise that an idea will succeed.
- Where a product has not published revenue, the record says so instead of estimating a figure.

## Research loop

1. **Search**: describe an idea, product or website.
2. **Compare**: see who is already solving the same problem.
3. **Follow**: track the changes that make a market legible.
