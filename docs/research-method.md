# Research method

A useful research record answers three questions: **what was observed, when it was checked, and what that evidence can support.**

## What a research record contains

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Observed object | The product, creator, category, or official page |
| Signal | Traffic estimate with its month, pricing captured from a public page, or revenue when a verified or disclosed source exists |
| Change to follow | Pricing, positioning, features, or first-screen design tokens, each with a date |
| Evidence | Source link, observation date, and the change itself |
| Boundary | What the record does not show, including undisclosed revenue |

Records are re-checked when the source moves. An observation stays separate from a conclusion drawn from it. EarlyLore's own pages ask for the same habit: keep the observation month, source, and scope on every cited figure.

## What the public product is for

- Compare an idea with live products, categories, pricing, creators, and source-linked changes.
- Read an evaluation report you already ran in your account, including the core bet, the dealbreakers, and the MVP prescription.
- Inspect first-screen Design DNA and niche pricing benchmarks while designing a page.
- Follow a creator's public portfolio and a product's public change history.

EarlyLore does not promise that an idea will succeed. Where a product has not published revenue, the record says so. Traffic is an estimate, not server logs from the product. Design DNA describes a captured first screen. It is not permission to copy another product's brand, copy, or assets.

The public changelog has described the evaluation pipeline as both 6-lane (2026-11-06) and 4-lane (2026-11-08). Use the live [/mcp](https://earlylore.com/mcp) page and the dated changelog entry when you need the current pipeline description. This method page does not freeze a lane count.

## Research loop

1. **Search.** Describe an idea, product, or website, or open the category and creator directories.
2. **Compare.** Read who already addresses the same job, with pricing, traffic month, and any disclosed revenue.
3. **Follow.** Track the dated changes that make the market legible, and save notes in the signed-in workspace when the account has that feature.
4. **Build from evidence.** When a coding agent is connected, use the hosted MCP server and [skill spec](https://earlylore.com/skill.md) so generated pages and prices stay tied to a source. Setup is in [mcp-and-skill.md](mcp-and-skill.md).
