# EarlyLore: research real SaaS products, revenue signals and competitor changes before you build

[EarlyLore](https://earlylore.com) helps founders and independent makers **validate SaaS ideas** against real products that already make money. It follows a product's **revenue evidence**, traffic, **pricing**, positioning and feature changes, and keeps the **source and observation date** next to every fact, so you can tell an observed fact from a conclusion.

EarlyLore is in **research preview**. The public site describes the research scope; the product opens later.

## What you can research

- **SaaS ideas worth validating**: compare a product direction with real SaaS products, user problems, pricing and visible market signals before you commit weeks to building.
- **Competitor monitoring**: follow public changes to positioning, pricing, features and key product pages, each with its source and observation date.
- **New product ideas**: start with a question, find the products and makers already answering it, compare the field and keep the evidence that changes your next decision.

The research loop is **Search → Compare → Follow**: describe an idea, product or website; see who already solves the same problem; track the changes that make a market legible.
